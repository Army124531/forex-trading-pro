Signal Kab Aur Kaise Banta Hai?

Signal ka koi fixed time nahi hai. BUY/SELL tabhi banta hai jab 6 gates me se 85% ya zyada points mil jayein. WAIT ka matlab hai: abhi trade mat karein, shartein poori nahi hain.

SMC ka gate alag hai (80%) — SMC tab me dekhein. Terminal ka signal SMC gate par nahi rukta.

SMC (Smart Money Concepts)

SMC ke levels asal 15M candles ke swing high/low se nikale jate hain — jahan bade kharidar/bechne wale aam tor par rukte hain. SMC score 5 hisse x 20 points se banta hai, gate 80% hai. Terminal ka BUY/SELL is tab se nahi banta; wo Terminal ki apni 85% wali sharton se banta hai.
