Signal Kab Aur Kaise Banta Hai?

Signal ka koi fixed time nahi hai. BUY/SELL tabhi banta hai jab 6 gates me se 85% ya zyada points mil jayein. WAIT ka matlab hai: abhi trade mat karein, shartein poori nahi hain.

SMC ka gate alag hai (80%) — SMC tab me dekhein. Terminal ka signal SMC gate par nahi rukta.

Best timeframe: 1H (4H trend ke saath) — 1H/4H par signals sab se reliable hote hain, 15M sirf entry timing ke liye hai, 5M par zyada noise hota hai. (90-din backtest, 4 pairs: win rate ~60%, profit factor ~1.00 — breakeven, pichla result hai, guarantee nahi.)

Signal kam aate hain, lekin behtar hote hain — BUY/SELL sirf tabhi dikhta hai jab 85% gate poora ho. Zyada signals ka matlab behtar result nahi hota.
